Simple 4-Step Process

How CivicFix Works

From citizen report to field officer execution, complete transparency at every milestone.

1

Report the Issue

Take a photo, pick your location on the map, and submit the complaint in under a minute.

2

Verify & Assign

Municipal scouts inspect validity and route the work order to the specific department officer.

3

Track the Progress

Receive in-app alerts as field crews deploy and monitor the live visual timeline.

4

Resolve the Issue

Field officers complete repairs and upload photographic completion proof.
